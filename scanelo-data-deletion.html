Delete your data

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Scanelo for Android (com.durusoft.pdfscanner) by DuruSoft

There is no Scanelo account

Scanelo has no sign-in and no servers of its own, so there is no account to delete. Your documents and everything the app keeps are on your phone, and you delete them there. The steps below also cover the few things kept elsewhere: backups you made, advertising data held by Google, purchase data held by RevenueCat, and emails you sent us.

1Delete single documents

  1. In Scanelo, delete the documents you no longer want. They move to the trash.
  2. To delete them for good right away, open Profile › Trash and empty it. Otherwise they are deleted automatically after 30 days.

2Delete everything on your phone

  1. If you have a subscription, cancel it first in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions or in Scanelo under Profile › Manage subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  2. Save or back up anything you want to keep. Locked documents cannot be recovered after the next step.
  3. Open Android Settings › Apps › Scanelo › Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletes all documents and pages, recognized text, translations, signatures, locked documents and your PIN, folders, tags, the trash, QR code history, settings, statistics, downloaded language packs and your last known plan.

Files you saved outside the app, for example in Downloads/Scanelo, and ZIP backups you made are not deleted with the app. Delete them in your Files app, on your SD card, or in the cloud service where you kept them.

3Android backup

If Android backup is on, only your Scanelo settings may be in your Google account backup; documents are never included. You can delete device backups in your Google account’s storage settings (Google One or Google Drive › Backups) or turn backup off in Android Settings.

4Advertising data (free version)

Google AdMob receives the data described in our Privacy Policy. To delete your advertising ID, go to Android Settings (on most phones Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID). Where your region requires it, you can also change or withdraw your ad consent in Scanelo under Profile › Ad privacy choices. Google keeps ad data under its own policy; you can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

5Purchase data

If you bought Scanelo Pro or No ads, RevenueCat keeps a random app user ID with your purchase history so your purchase can be restored. To have it deleted, email us at duru.sofft@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Scanelo purchase data” and your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.” and is in the receipt email from Google Play), so we can find the right record. We will delete it from RevenueCat, usually within 30 days, and confirm by email. After that, “Restore purchases” cannot bring that purchase back in Scanelo. Google Play keeps your order history in your Google account under Google’s policy.

6Emails to us

Ask us to delete our email conversation with you at duru.sofft@gmail.com. Otherwise we delete it when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after our last exchange.

7Text recognition and translation data

Scanelo never sends your documents or their text anywhere. The diagnostic data that Google’s ML Kit receives when it delivers and runs the on-device models (see our Privacy Policy) is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
