2What the app does

Water Eject plays tones, frequency sweeps and pulses through your phone’s loudspeaker and earpiece (and, in some modes, your headphones), optionally together with vibration. Sound pressure moves the speaker membrane, which can help push out water and loosen dust caught in the speaker grille. The app also includes a tone generator, a speaker test, a microphone test and a dB meter.

The animations in the app show how the method works. They are illustrations and do not mean that you will see water coming out of your phone.

3Safe use

Protect your hearing. Cleaning uses loud, low and high tones at high volume, and the tone generator can play up to 20,000 Hz. Never hold the phone against or close to your ear while a cleaning, test or tone is playing, and do not play tones through headphones you are wearing at high volume.
  • Hold or place the phone with the speaker facing down, as the app suggests, and keep it away from your ears, children and pets. Some animals can hear high tones that people cannot.
  • Stop at once if the speaker rattles, distorts, gets warm, or the phone behaves unusually. The app adds a short rest between longer sessions to protect the speaker; please do not work around it by running sessions back to back for a long time.
  • Water Eject does not dry a phone that has been submerged. If your phone has been under water, follow the manufacturer’s guidance (for example, switch it off and let it dry) and have it checked if it shows signs of damage. Do not put your phone in water to test the app.
  • Do not use the app where loud sounds could startle others or are not allowed, or where you need your full attention, such as while driving.
