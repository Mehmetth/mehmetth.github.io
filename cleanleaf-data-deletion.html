Delete your data

Effective: October 27, 2026 · CleanLeaf for Android (com.durusoft.storage.cleaner.cleanleaf) by DuruSoft

There is no CleanLeaf account

CleanLeaf has no sign-in and no servers of its own, so there is no account to delete. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, and you delete it there.

1Delete everything on your phone

  1. If you have a subscription, cancel it first in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions (deleting the app does not cancel it).
  2. Export anything you want to keep from the private vault. Vault items cannot be recovered after the next step.
  3. Open Android Settings › Apps › CleanLeaf › Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletes scan results, selections, statistics, trash records, contact backups, the vault, settings and your last known Premium status. Photos already in the trash stay in Android’s trash until it empties them (30 days) or you restore or delete them in your gallery app.

2Device backup

If Android backup is on, your CleanLeaf settings may be in your Google account backup. You can delete backups in Google Drive › Backups or turn backup off in Android Settings.

3Advertising data (free plan)

Google AdMob receives your advertising ID and related data. You can delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID, and change ad choices in the app under Me › Settings › Privacy & ads › Privacy options (where available). Google keeps ad data under its own policy.

4Purchase data

RevenueCat keeps a random app user ID with your purchase history so Premium can be restored. To have it deleted, email us from the address you want us to answer, with the subject “Delete my CleanLeaf purchase data”. We will ask RevenueCat to delete it, usually within 30 days. Google Play keeps your order history in your Google account.
