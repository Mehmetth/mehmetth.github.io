4Deleted messages: what it can and cannot do

The “Deleted messages” feature keeps copies of WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business messages that your phone shows as notifications, so you can still read a message after the sender deletes it.

  • It works only after you turn on notification access, and only for messages that arrived as notifications while the feature was on.
  • It cannot recover messages from WhatsApp, from before you turned it on, from chats you had open or muted, from notifications that hide the message text, or that arrived while access was off. A deleted message can only be read if its notification arrived first.
  • Photo previews are kept only with Premium and only when the notification shows one.
  • The free version keeps the last 24 hours. With Premium, messages are kept until you clear them; if Premium ends, messages older than 24 hours are deleted.
  • It depends on how WhatsApp and Android show notifications, which they may change at any time. We therefore cannot guarantee that every message is kept, or that the feature keeps working.

5The vault and your files

  • Files in the vault are kept only on your phone and have no other copy. Deleting them in the vault, clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes them for good, and we cannot bring them back.
  • If your phone has no screen lock, the vault uses a 4-digit vault PIN. It cannot be recovered: if you forget it, we cannot open the vault for you.
  • The app’s own data is not included in Android’s cloud backup. Moving the vault back to the gallery before you change or reset your phone is your responsibility.
  • Copies you save go to your gallery (the StatusSaver album). They stay there if you uninstall the app.
