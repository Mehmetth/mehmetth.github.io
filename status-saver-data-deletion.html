Delete your data

Effective: 18 November 2026 · Status Saver for Android (com.durusoft.statussaver.statusdownloader.downloadstatus.storysaver) by DuruSoft

There is no Status Saver account

Status Saver has no sign-in and no servers of its own, so there is no account to delete. Your statuses, saved copies, vault, kept messages, stickers and Status Studio images stay on your phone, and you delete them there (step 1). A few services we use receive limited data; steps 2 to 4 show how to stop or delete it. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

1Delete everything on your phone

  1. If you have Premium as a subscription, cancel it first in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  2. Move anything you want to keep out of the vault with Back to gallery. Vault items cannot be recovered after the next step.
  3. Open Android Settings › Apps › Status Saver › Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletes: the vault and its PIN, kept messages and their photo previews, sticker packs, recent Direct chat numbers, the list of statuses already seen or saved, settings, your Sparks and your last known Premium status.

What stays: copies you saved to your gallery (Pictures/StatusSaver and Movies/StatusSaver) are ordinary files; delete them in your gallery or file manager. Stickers you already added to WhatsApp stay in WhatsApp until you remove them there. The app's data is never included in Android's cloud backup.

2Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Turn off Profile › Share usage statistics: nothing more is recorded or sent. Data already sent is linked only to a random app instance ID, not to your name, phone number or account, and Google deletes it automatically: usage events after at most 14 months, crash reports after at most 90 days. Clearing the app's storage (step 1) also gives the app a new random ID.

3Advertising data (Google AdMob, free version)

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • In Türkiye: turn off Profile › Personalised ads (Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar).
  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws: change your choice in Profile › Ad privacy choices.
  • Premium shows no ads, so no ad request is made.

Google keeps advertising data under its own policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4Purchase records (Google Play, RevenueCat)

If you bought Premium, RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase linked to a random ID so Premium can be restored. To delete it, write to duru.sofft@gmail.com with the subject “Status Saver data deletion” and your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.; find it in the purchase e-mail from Google Play or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history).

What is kept: after deletion, a restored purchase can no longer be matched to the old record. Google Play keeps its own order records, and records that tax, accounting or consumer laws require are kept for the period those laws set.

5Ask us

For any other request about your data, or if you are unsure, write to duru.sofft@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Messages you send us are deleted at the latest two years after our last exchange; tell us if you want yours deleted sooner.
