Information We Handle

The short version

Your photos and your history stay on your iPhone. The only data that leaves the device is handled by Google AdMob (ads, free version) and by Apple and RevenueCat (purchases).

02

How We Use Information

  • Compress, resize, crop and convert the photos you pick
  • Remember your settings and show your history
  • Check whether you have SnapSize Pro, through Apple and RevenueCat
  • Show ads in the free version, through Google AdMob

We do not build profiles, sell data, or share it beyond what sections 4 and 5 describe.

03

Photos Access

Your photos never leave your device

All processing uses Apple's ImageIO on your iPhone. No photo is uploaded to any server.

  • System photo picker — you choose photos with Apple's picker. SnapSize sees only the photos you pick and cannot browse your library. No permission is needed for this.
  • Add Photos Only — to save the smaller copies to Photos. SnapSize cannot read or browse your existing photos.
  • Share from Photos — when you share photos to SnapSize from the Photos app, they are processed the same way, on your iPhone.

Your originals are never changed. Location data (GPS) is always removed from the copies. The date taken and camera model are kept unless you turn this off in SnapSize Settings.

You can change the permission anytime at Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SnapSize.

04

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. SnapSize Pro shows no ads.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) — Google's consent form is shown first. You can change your choice later in SnapSize → Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • App Tracking Transparency — SnapSize asks once. If you allow it, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalised ads. If you do not, ads are still shown without it.

Under its own policy, Google AdMob may collect device information (model, OS version), IP address, the advertising identifier (only with your permission) and ad interaction data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps.

05

In-App Purchase

SnapSize Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store: no ads, up to 100 photos at a time and exact file size for batches. It is not a subscription and does not renew. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

All payments are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

If you subscribed to an earlier weekly or yearly plan, you keep Pro while that subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it at Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

06

Data Storage & Security

  • Settings and history are stored in the app's sandbox on your iPhone and are not synced to iCloud by SnapSize
  • Working copies are written to a temporary folder and cleaned up automatically
  • The SnapSize widget and share extension read the same settings through the App Group group.com.durusoft.snapsize

iOS sandboxing keeps other apps from reading SnapSize's data.

07

Children's Privacy

SnapSize is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with a general-audience content rating.

08

Your Choices

  • Delete history — History → Select → Delete in the app. Copies already saved to Photos stay in Photos.
  • Delete all data — uninstalling the app removes everything it stored
  • Photos access — Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SnapSize
  • Tracking — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) — SnapSize → Settings → Ad privacy settings

If you believe we hold personal data about you (for example from a support email) and want it deleted, contact us below.
