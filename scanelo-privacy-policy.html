Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Applies to: Scanelo for Android (com.durusoft.pdfscanner), version 1.0 and later

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning, text recognition, translation and the PDF tools run on your device. Scanelo never uploads your pages, images, text or files.
  • No account, no servers of our own. We do not ask for your name or email, and we cannot see your documents or how you use the app.
  • On the free version, Google AdMob shows ads, with the consent your region requires. Ads never appear while you scan. With Scanelo Pro or the No ads purchase, the ads software is not started.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. RevenueCat tells the app what you bought using a random ID. We never see your card details.
  • Google delivers the text recognition, barcode and translation models and the language packs to your phone. Google’s ML Kit receives basic diagnostic data, never your documents.
  • We do not use an analytics or crash-reporting service, and we do not sell your personal data.

1Who we are

Scanelo is an Android app published on Google Play by DuruSoft, based in Türkiye (“we”, “us”). For the limited personal data described here that we decide about, we are the data controller. Google acts as a separate controller or as our processor for the Google services described below, and RevenueCat acts as our processor for purchases. You can reach us at duru.sofft@gmail.com.

3What stays on your phone

Scanelo keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. We cannot access it.

  • Your documents and pages, with your edits, markup and up to three saved signatures on the free version (unlimited with Pro).
  • Recognized text of every page, used for search, automatic names (for example “Invoice”, “Statement” or “Contract” with the sender’s name) and smart folders. It is made on your phone and never sent.
  • Folders, tags, favorites and the trash. Deleted documents stay in the trash for 30 days, then are deleted for good.
  • Locked documents. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key kept in the Android Keystore of your phone. They are left out of search and previews.
  • QR code history, settings, statistics and your last known plan, so Pro works offline.
  • Widgets. The recent documents widget shows the names of up to three recent documents on your home screen. Locked documents are never shown.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this data. It does not remove files you saved outside the app (for example in Downloads/Scanelo) or ZIP backups you made; you delete those yourself.

4Permissions and why

Permissions that need your OK are asked for only when you use the feature that needs them. You can refuse or withdraw them in Android Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: to scan documents, ID cards and passports and to read QR codes and barcodes. Photos are processed on your phone.
  • Notifications (optional): a reminder two days before a free trial turns into a paid plan, a notice if an automatic backup could not be written, and a weekly backup reminder if you turn it on.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): to open locked documents or the app lock. Android checks your fingerprint or face; Scanelo only learns whether the check succeeded.
  • Internet and network state: to download models and language packs, for purchases, and for ads on the free version.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Scanelo Pro or No ads.
  • Advertising ID and Android’s ad services (advertising ID, ad attribution and ad topics, where your Android version supports them): used by Google’s ads software on the free version only.
  • Foreground service (data sync): lets Google Play finish unpacking a large language pack while Scanelo shows its progress.
  • Keep the phone awake (wake lock): lets background work finish, such as reading the text of new scans or an automatic backup.

Scanelo does not ask for storage, photo library, location or contacts permissions. When you import a photo or a PDF, or choose where to save or back up, you pick it in Android’s own photo picker or file picker, and Scanelo receives only what you picked.

5Text recognition, barcodes and translation

Text recognition (OCR), the QR code and barcode scanner and the optional translation tool run on your phone using Google ML Kit. Your pages, images and text are never sent to Google or to us.

  • The models for Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari text and for barcodes are downloaded once by Google Play services.
  • Language packs for Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Pashto, Russian, Ukrainian, Uzbek (Cyrillic), Greek, Thai, Bengali and Khmer text are downloaded from Google Play only when you ask for them.
  • The translation tool is downloaded from Google Play the first time you use it, and each language model is downloaded from Google the first time you translate between two languages.

To run these features, Google receives the data listed in section 2. According to Google, ML Kit uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit and does not share it with third parties; for translation, ML Kit uses Firebase Installations and Firebase Remote Config for remote configuration and diagnostics. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

“Read aloud” uses the text-to-speech engine you chose in Android. The engine is provided by Android or by its maker under their own terms.

6Advertising and your consent

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear while you use the camera, crop or filters, and they never use your documents or their text. Formats are ads in lists, a full-screen ad after some saves, an ad when you come back to the app after a while, a banner in some countries, and, only if you choose it, a short video to use a Pro tool once. We ask Google to show only ads rated for a general audience with parental guidance (rating “PG”) or lower.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before ads are requested. “Do not consent” is a valid answer and the app works the same. Where the rules apply to you, you can change your choice at any time in Profile › Ad privacy choices or Profile › Privacy › Ad choices. Google may collect the data listed in section 2 to show, measure and protect ads; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

With Scanelo Pro or No ads, the ads software is not started and receives nothing; Google’s consent tool may still check, when the app starts, whether a consent form is required in your region. In countries where Scanelo cannot sell through Google Play, no ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (on most phones Settings › Privacy › Ads).

7Purchases

Scanelo Pro (yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) and No ads (a one-time purchase) are sold through Google Play. Google handles payment and your payment details; we never see them. RevenueCat checks with Google Play what you bought, using a random app user ID that is not linked to your name or email, and receives the purchase information listed in section 2.

So that a purchase can come back after you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, RevenueCat may store that random ID in Google’s Block Store on your phone. Google Play services can include it in your device backup, end-to-end encrypted on Android 9 and later when your phone has a screen lock. It contains no name, email or document. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

8Usage statistics and crash reports

This version of Scanelo does not use an analytics or crash-reporting service and sends no usage statistics to us or to anyone else. The switch Profile › Privacy › Share anonymous usage statistics controls whether the app records anonymous usage events on your phone (which screens and tools are used, never document names or content); in this version these records are not transmitted. Before we ever send such statistics, we will update this policy, and they will be sent only while the switch is on.

If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports.

9Backups

Android backup. If Android backup is on, only your Scanelo settings are included. Documents, recognized text, signatures, locked documents and your PIN are never included.

Your own ZIP backups. In Profile › Backup you can save all documents, folders, tags and signatures as one ZIP file in a place you choose: your phone, an SD card or a cloud folder such as Google Drive. With Pro, automatic backup can write it daily to a folder you chose, keeping the last three. Locked documents stay encrypted in the backup and open only with your security PIN. If you choose a cloud folder, that provider stores the file under its own terms; Scanelo does not upload it anywhere else.

10Files you share, save or print

When you share a document, Scanelo sends the file itself to the app you pick; it adds no link, watermark or “scanned with” text. Saved files go to Downloads/Scanelo on Android 10 and later, or to the place you choose on older Android, where other apps can see them as for any download. Temporary copies made for sharing are deleted after a day. Printing goes through Android’s print service and the printer you choose. When a QR code contains a link, Scanelo shows the address before opening it in your browser; Wi-Fi details and contacts from a QR code are handed to Android only when you tap to use them.

11When you contact us

If you write to us (for example from Profile › Help and feedback › Report a problem), your email app sends us your email address and what you write. If you choose “Add phone details”, the message also includes your Scanelo version, Android version and API level, and the phone’s maker and model; you can also attach a screenshot you pick. We use this only to answer you and fix the issue you report, and we delete it when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after our last exchange.

12Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data reaches only the services named in this policy, for the purposes stated. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

13Retention and deletion

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Documents in the trash are deleted for good after 30 days, or earlier if you empty the trash. Google and RevenueCat keep data according to their own policies. How to delete everything: Delete your data.

14Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, to object to its use and to withdraw consent. If you are in Türkiye, you have the rights in KVKK Article 11. Because we hold almost nothing about you, most of these are exercised on your phone: delete documents, clear or uninstall the app, change your ad choices, or reset your advertising ID. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, write to us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

15Children

Scanelo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

16Security

Your documents are processed on your phone. Locked documents are encrypted on the phone, screens showing them cannot be captured in screenshots, and connections to Google and RevenueCat are encrypted in transit. No method is perfect, but we keep what leaves your phone to the minimum.

17International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in other countries, including the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

18Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update the effective date above, and for important changes we tell you in the app before they apply.
