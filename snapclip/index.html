Features

Privacy

On your phone, not on a server

  • No account needed. Your saved videos, photos and sounds, your library and your vault stay on your phone. SnapClip has no servers of its own and uploads none of them.
  • To find the file, the link you save is sent to a third-party video information service. Nothing else about you or your files is sent.
  • Ads on the free plan come from Google AdMob, with your consent choice where the law requires it. Premium shows no ads.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. SnapClip has no sign-in. Your library, saved files and vault stay on your phone.

Where are my saved videos?

In your phone’s gallery (videos) and music folder (MP3), and in SnapClip’s built-in library, which has separate Video and Music tabs.

Why can’t I save a video?

Private, deleted or region-restricted videos cannot be saved, and an incomplete link will not work. Copy the full link again and retry. Saving also depends on a third-party video information service, so it can be briefly unavailable.

What does Premium add?

Premium removes ads and adds MP3 and ringtones, HD video, batch download of up to 10 links and the PIN-protected vault.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose SnapClip and cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is it okay to save any video?

Save only content you own or have permission to use, and respect creators’ rights and each platform’s rules. SnapClip is a tool for personal use. See the Terms and Conditions.
