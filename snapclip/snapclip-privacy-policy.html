3What stays on your device

SnapClip works on your device. We cannot access any of the following, and the app does not upload it:

  • Saved videos, photos and sounds are written to your device’s gallery and music folders.
  • Your library: a local list of what you saved (titles, creators, links, favourites) so the library can show it.
  • Vault: files you move into the vault are kept in the app’s private storage, behind your PIN (and your fingerprint if you turn it on; Android handles the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded). The PIN is stored only as a salted hash. The vault never leaves your device.
  • Settings, streak and counters are stored on the device only.

Uninstalling removes this data, including the vault. Vault files cannot be recovered, so move out anything you want to keep before you uninstall or reset the vault.

4Links you save

When you paste or share a video link, the app sends that link to a third-party video information service (by default tikwm.com) to get the video’s details and file addresses. Like any internet request, this reveals your device’s IP address to that service. The service is operated independently of DuruSoft and has its own privacy practices. The video files are then downloaded from the addresses the service returns. Only the link is sent: not your files, library, vault or any personal details.

DuruSoft may route these requests through its own proxy (a Cloudflare Worker) when that is switched on in the app’s remote settings. The proxy forwards the link to the video information service and keeps the public answer for that link in Cloudflare’s cache for up to 10 minutes, so repeated requests for the same video are faster. If request limiting is switched on, your IP address is used only to count requests. The proxy writes no logs of its own.

5Clipboard

SnapClip reads the clipboard only while the app is open on its home screen, and only to look for a supported video link. Android may show a “pasted from your clipboard” message when this happens. Clipboard contents are not stored or sent anywhere, except a supported link as described in section 4.

6Permissions and why

  • Internet and network state: to look up links, download files, show ads, buy Premium and read remote settings.
  • Notifications (optional): download progress and “ready” alerts, and optional reminders such as a free-trial reminder. They are created on your device.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save files to your gallery and music folders. Android 10 and later saves through the system media store and needs no storage permission.
  • Modify system settings (optional): only if you choose to set a sound you saved as your ringtone.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): to open the vault.

You can refuse or withdraw any optional permission in your device’s Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

7Advertising and your consent

On the free plan, SnapClip shows ads from Google AdMob. The ad formats may include App Open, Interstitial, Rewarded and Native ads (and banners where shown). Rewarded ads are always your choice. Ads never use your saved videos, files or library.

AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location it reveals, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This can include personalized ads, which are based on your interests and activity.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where required in US states, SnapClip asks for your choice through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form (GDPR and similar laws) before ads are personalized or requested; “Do not consent” is a valid answer and the app works the same, with non-personalized ads where the law allows ads at all. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy settings. You can also turn off ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (the exact path depends on your device) and in your Google ad settings. Premium users see no ads.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

8Purchases and subscriptions

Premium (monthly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime purchase) is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes your payment and keeps your payment details and order history in your Google account; we never receive or store your card or other payment information. The app only learns whether you own Premium, so it can remove ads and unlock Premium features, and it can restore your purchase on any device signed in with the same Google account. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

9Other services

  • Remote settings: the app downloads a small settings file from GitHub Pages (mehmetth.github.io) to learn a few settings and the latest version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request. No data about you is sent with it.
  • Google Play in-app review and in-app update prompts may be shown by Google Play, under Google’s own policy.
  • Crash reports: we use no crash-reporting service. If you allow your device to share diagnostics with app developers, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance data.

10Device backup

If Android backup is on, your SnapClip settings may be included. Vault files, the vault PIN hash and the library list are excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

11When you contact us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use this only to answer you and fix the issue you report, and delete it when no longer needed, at the latest two years after our last exchange.

12Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data reaches only the services named in this policy, for the purposes stated, and we work only with services that commit to protecting it at least as well as this policy and the store rules require. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

13Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Google and the video information service keep data under their own policies. There is no SnapClip account or server-side data to delete. How to delete everything, reset your advertising ID and cancel a subscription: Delete your data.

14Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. If you are in Türkiye, you have the rights in KVKK Article 11. Because we hold almost nothing about you, most of these are done on your device: delete items or clear or uninstall the app, change ad choices in Settings › Privacy settings, or reset your advertising ID. For data held by Google or the video information service, write to us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

15Children

SnapClip is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

16Security

Your files, library and vault are kept on your device, and the vault PIN is stored only as a salted hash. Connections to the services above use encryption in transit. No method is perfect, but we keep what leaves your device to the minimum.
