Delete your data

Effective: November 20, 2026 (2026-11-20) · SnapClip for Android (com.durusoft.snapclip.video.downloader.nowatermark.saver.videodownloader.withoutwatermark) by DuruSoft

There is no SnapClip account

  • SnapClip has no sign-in and no servers of its own, so we hold no account and no user data on a server. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.
  • Everything the app keeps is on your device, and you delete it there. Deleting it is immediate and permanent: nobody can restore it, including us.

1Cancel your subscription first (if you have one)

Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a subscription. To stop future charges:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  3. Choose SnapClip, tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps.

Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. If you are on a free trial, cancel before it ends to avoid a charge. A lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

2Delete your files inside the app

You can delete selected data without removing the app:

  1. Vault: open the vault with your PIN, select the files and delete them. To remove the whole vault at once, use Forgot PIN? and confirm; this deletes every vault file and removes the PIN. Vault files cannot be recovered.
  2. Library: open the library, select one or more items and tap Delete. This also removes the saved files from your gallery.

3Delete all app data from your device

  1. Export or copy anything you want to keep, especially from the vault.
  2. Open Android Settings › Apps › SnapClip › Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data). This deletes your library list, settings, streak and counters, vault files, the vault PIN and your last known Premium status.
  3. Or uninstall the app (Settings › Apps › SnapClip › Uninstall). This deletes the same data.

Videos, photos and sounds you saved to your gallery or music folder are ordinary files on your phone. Delete them in your gallery or file manager app (or from the SnapClip library, step 2) if you no longer want them.

4Device backup

If Android backup is on, your SnapClip settings may be stored in your Google account backup. (Vault files, the vault PIN and the library list are excluded from backups.) You can delete backups in Google Drive › Backups or turn backup off in Android Settings.

5Reset or delete your advertising ID (free plan)

Google AdMob receives the data described in our Privacy Policy. To control it:

  1. Open Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some devices Settings › Google › Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID) and confirm. You can also turn off ad personalization there.
  3. In the app, open Settings › Privacy settings to review or change your consent choice (Google UMP) where it is offered.

Google keeps ad data under its own policy. You can manage it at Google My Ad Center. Premium shows no ads.

6Purchase and payment records

Google Play keeps your order history and payment details in your Google account under its own policy; we never receive them. Manage or delete them in your Google account (myaccount.google.com) or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

7Links you saved, and services we use

When you save a video, the link is sent to a third-party video information service (and, if switched on, our Cloudflare Worker proxy, which keeps only the public answer for that link for up to 10 minutes and writes no logs). We cannot link those requests to you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete. To ask the video information service about its own records, use its privacy contact.

8Emails to us

Ask us to delete our email conversation with you at duru.sofft@gmail.com. We will do it, usually within 30 days.
