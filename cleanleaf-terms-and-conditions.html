2What the app does

CleanLeaf helps you free storage on your device. All of it happens on your device.

  • On both platforms: it finds duplicate, similar and blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, lets you sort photos by swiping, and can compress videos.
  • On Android it also finds large and duplicate files, unused apps and duplicate contacts, and can keep chosen photos in an encrypted private vault.
  • On iPhone and iPad it also finds bursts and Live Photos, can turn a Live Photo into a still photo, and offers widgets and Siri shortcuts. iOS does not let apps read other apps’ files, list installed apps or clean system storage, so CleanLeaf does not offer those features there.

3Your decisions and deleted items

  • Suggestions such as “best shot”, “similar” or “blurry” are made by automatic analysis and can be wrong. Check before you confirm.
  • On Android, photos and videos you clean are moved to the trash (Android’s trash, or the app’s own on Android 10 and earlier). They can be restored for 30 days, after which they are deleted for good. If you turn on “Delete permanently” or empty the trash, they cannot be restored.
  • On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can restore them for 30 days. The space they take is freed when Recently Deleted is emptied, by you or by iOS after 30 days; CleanLeaf cannot empty it for you.
  • If your photos are also in a cloud service, that service follows its own rules. With iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo on one device deletes it on all your devices that use iCloud Photos.
  • On Android, merging or deleting contacts changes them in your contacts account; the app keeps a local backup so you can undo it, as described in the app. Uninstalling apps from CleanLeaf is done by Android after you confirm each one, and removes that app’s data.
  • After a video is compressed, or a Live Photo turned into a still photo (iPhone and iPad), the new version is saved to your photo library. The original goes to the trash or to Recently Deleted only if you chose that and the system confirmation is accepted, and can be restored there for 30 days; otherwise it stays where it was.

4Private vault (Android)

Vault items are encrypted on your device. We cannot open them or recover them for you. If you forget your PIN and lose your recovery code, the items cannot be recovered. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the vault; export what you want to keep first. On the free plan the vault holds up to 10 items. If Premium ends, everything already in the vault stays and still opens; you can add more again with Premium, or on the free plan once you are under 10.
