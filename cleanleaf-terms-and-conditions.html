2What the app does

CleanLeaf helps you free storage on your phone: it finds duplicate, similar and blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and files, unused apps and duplicate contacts; it can compress videos and keep chosen photos in an encrypted private vault. All of this happens on your device.

3Your decisions and deleted items

  • Suggestions such as “best shot”, “similar” or “blurry” are made by automatic analysis and can be wrong. Check before you confirm.
  • Photos and videos you clean are moved to the trash (Android’s trash, or the app’s own on Android 10 and earlier). They can be restored for 30 days, after which they are deleted for good. If you turn on “Delete permanently” or empty the trash, they cannot be restored.
  • If your photos are also in a cloud service, that service follows its own rules: copies there may stay or be removed too.
  • Merging or deleting contacts changes them in your contacts account; the app keeps a local backup so you can undo it, as described in the app.
  • Uninstalling apps from CleanLeaf is done by Android after you confirm each one, and removes that app’s data.
  • After a video is compressed, its original goes to the trash only when Android’s confirmation is accepted, and can be restored there for 30 days; otherwise it stays where it was.

If Premium ends, everything already in the vault stays and still opens; you can add more again with Premium, or on the free plan once you are under 10.
