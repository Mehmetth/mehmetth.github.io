Delete Your Data

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Video Compressor for Android (com.durusoft.videocompressor.size.reducer.converter) by DuruSoft

In short

  • There is no account. Video Compressor keeps your videos, history and settings only on your device, so most data is deleted right on your phone.
  • Ad data is controlled with the in-app ad privacy choices and your Android advertising ID.
  • Purchase records kept by our subscription service can be deleted on request by email. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

1. Videos and history on your device

Delete one item from the history:

  1. Open the History tab in Video Compressor.
  2. Tap More options on an item, then Delete, and confirm. The item is removed from the app.

Delete the video or audio files the app created: open your gallery or the Files app and delete them from Movies/VideoCompressor (videos) or Music/VideoCompressor (audio). Deleting a history item does not delete these gallery copies.

Delete an original video: on the result screen, tap Delete Original and confirm, or delete it in your gallery.

Delete all app data at once (history, settings and temporary files):

  1. Open Android Settings › Apps › Video Compressor.
  2. Tap Storage (or Storage & cache), then Clear data (or Clear storage).
  3. Or tap Uninstall to remove the app and all of its private data.

Clearing data or uninstalling does not delete the videos already saved to Movies/VideoCompressor, so you keep your results. Delete them in your gallery if you want them gone too.

2. Advertising ID and consent choices

Change or withdraw your ad consent (shown where the law requires a consent choice, such as in the EEA and the UK):

  1. Open Video Compressor › Settings.
  2. Tap Ad privacy choices and change your choice. Clearing the app's data also resets your stored consent, and the app asks again.

Delete or reset your advertising ID:

  1. Open Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones: Google › Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID (Android 12 and later) or Reset advertising ID.

You can also manage ad personalization in My Ad Center. Data that Google has already collected through ads is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, where you can also request deletion from your Google account.

3. Subscription and purchase records: deletion request

Video Compressor has no analytics and no account, so there are no usage or profile records to delete. If you bought Pro, our subscription service RevenueCat keeps a purchase record linked to an anonymous ID. To have it deleted:

  1. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription.
  2. Send an email to duru.sofft@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request — Video Compressor.
  3. Include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt email) so we can find the record. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

What happens next: we confirm your request within 7 days and delete the purchase record within 30 days at the latest, then confirm by email. We may keep a minimal record of the request itself, and Google may keep order records it must keep for tax and legal reasons under its own policy. After deletion, Pro can no longer be restored to a device through the app.

Need more help?

Questions about your data? Write to DuruSoft at duru.sofft@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
