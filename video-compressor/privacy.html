Privacy Policy

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not ask for your name or email to use the app.

1Who we are

Video Compressor is an Android app published on Google Play by DuruSoft, based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For the limited personal data described here that we decide about, we are the data controller. Google and RevenueCat act as separate controllers or as our processors for the services they provide. You can reach us at duru.sofft@gmail.com.

3Your videos stay on your device

All video processing uses Android's built-in Media3 framework and the hardware encoders of your phone. We cannot access any of the following, and the app does not upload it:

  • Videos you pick. You choose videos with the Android system photo picker, or share them to the app from another app. The app receives only those videos; it has no permission to read your gallery.
  • Results. New videos are saved to Movies/VideoCompressor and extracted audio to Music/VideoCompressor on your device. Temporary working files stay in the app's private cache and are removed by the app or by Android.
  • History. A list of past jobs (file name, sizes, date and status) is kept in the app's local database so you can find your results again.
  • Settings. Theme, default target, codec choice and similar preferences are stored locally.

Your original video is never changed. It is deleted only if you tap Delete Original on the result screen and confirm.

4Location and capture date in videos

Video files can contain the place and time they were recorded. Video Compressor handles this metadata only on your device:

  • Capture date is always kept in the new file.
  • Location follows your choice in Settings › Location in output videos: removed for sharing targets (the default), always removed, or always kept. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.
  • The app does not ask for permission to read media location. The Android photo picker may hide location before the app sees the file.

5Permissions and why

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: for ads, purchases and the update check. Videos are never sent over the internet.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional): progress and completion notifications, created on your device.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING: to keep compressing in the background, even with the screen off. WAKE_LOCK is used by Android's WorkManager for the same purpose.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 8 and 9 only): to save results to your gallery on older Android versions. Newer versions need no storage permission.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: to process purchases through Google Play.
  • AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*: the advertising ID and Android's Privacy Sandbox, used by AdMob on the free plan.

The app has no permission to read your photos, videos or files. You can refuse or withdraw notifications at any time in Android Settings without losing any compression feature.

6Advertising and your consent

On the free plan, Video Compressor shows ads from Google AdMob: banner, native, interstitial and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice, for example to unlock a larger batch or the HEVC and AV1 codecs once. No ads are shown while a video is compressing. Ads never use your videos, file names or history.

AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location it reveals, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This can include personalized ads based on your interests.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where required elsewhere, the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are personalized. "Do not consent" is a valid answer and the app works the same, with non-personalized or limited ads where the law allows them. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads), and manage ad personalization in My Ad Center. Pro users see no ads.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

7Purchases and subscriptions

Video Compressor Pro (weekly, monthly or yearly) is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes your payment; we never receive or store your card or payment details.

To check and restore your Pro status, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created on your device, the product you bought, the Google Play purchase token and subscription state, the store country and currency, basic device and app information, and your IP address. It does not receive your name, email address or videos, and we do not use this data for advertising.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

8Other services

  • Update check. The app downloads a small version file from our website on GitHub Pages to tell you when an update is available. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and the app version; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Ratings. The app may show Google Play's in-app review window. Google handles your rating; we only see public reviews.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, Android sends the file to the app you choose. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK.

9Device backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's local job history and settings in your device backup. Your Pro status and ad counters are excluded. The videos saved in Movies/VideoCompressor are ordinary gallery files, so your own gallery or cloud photo backup settings decide whether they are backed up.

10When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, including the app version and device model that the app adds to support emails. We use this only to answer you and fix the issue you report, and we delete it when it is no longer needed, at the latest two years after our last exchange.

11Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data reaches only the services named in this policy, for the purposes stated. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

12Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google, RevenueCat and GitHub keep data under their own policies. For step-by-step instructions to delete your data, cancel a subscription, reset your advertising ID or ask us to delete purchase records, see Delete Your Data.

13Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. If you are in Türkiye, you have the rights listed in KVKK Article 11. Because we hold very little about you, most of these actions are done on your device. For data held by RevenueCat or Google, write to us and we will help you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

14Children

Video Compressor is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

15Security

Your videos are processed and stored on your device. Connections to the services named here use HTTPS. No method is perfect, but we keep what leaves your device to the minimum described in this policy.

16International transfers

Google, RevenueCat and GitHub may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Your videos are never transferred anywhere.

17Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
