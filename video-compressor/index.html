FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Video Compressor free?

Yes. You can compress unlimited single videos with no watermark. Pro removes ads, lifts the batch limit and unlocks HEVC and AV1 without watching an ad.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. All compression and editing runs on your phone. Your videos never go to a server, and the app also works offline.

Can the result be bigger than my original?

No. If a video is already efficiently compressed and the result would be larger, the app keeps your original instead.

Will my video lose quality?

Smart compression keeps visual clarity and lowers the resolution only when the target size really needs it. Encode a 5-second preview and compare before and after on the same frame before you start.

Which target size should I choose?

16 MB for messaging apps, 10 MB for chat communities and group servers, 25 MB for email attachments, or type any custom size.

Does it keep working with the screen off?

Yes. Compression continues in the background with the screen off, and a notification tells you when it is finished.

Where are my compressed videos saved?

In Movies/VideoCompressor on your phone, and extracted audio in Music/VideoCompressor. Your original video stays where it was.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Video Compressor and tap Cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
