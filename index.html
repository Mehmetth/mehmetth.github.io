Three ways to scroll

Your script moves the way you talk

Pick the scrolling mode that suits each take, right from the teleprompter screen.

Fixed speed

Free

Smooth scrolling at the speed you set, in words per minute. See the time left and check your video length against your target platform.

Voice scrolling

Free · works offline

The text moves while you speak and waits when you pause. It listens for your voice, not your words, so it works in any language without internet.

Word tracking

Premium

The text follows you word by word as you read, even while recording. Uses your device's speech recognition, which may need internet.

Features

Everything you need to film in one take

Record in one tap

The camera opens with your script on top. A short countdown and you're rolling. Front or rear camera, portrait or landscape, focus and exposure lock, stabilization and a framing grid. Optionally stop at the last line.

Hands-free control

Bluetooth keyboards, presentation clickers and foot pedals work right away, and you can assign your own keys. Use the volume buttons or voice commands: "continue", "stop", "start over", "next paragraph".

Text mode for any rig

Use Prompta without the camera: read while filming with another camera, on teleprompter glass (mirror or flip the text) or while presenting live. Lock the screen to avoid accidental taps.

Write, paste or import

Import TXT, Markdown or Word (DOCX) files. Headings become sections you can jump between with one tap. Autosave and version history let you restore any earlier draft.

Easy to read, your way

Choose the font, weight, size, line and letter spacing, text color, background dimming, side margins and reading line position. Light and dark theme.

Speaks your language

The app is available in 36 languages. Search your scripts and recordings, and set an optional recording reminder on the days you choose.

Volume buttons: on iPhone and iPad with the camera on (iOS 17.2+). Voice commands and word tracking on Android need Android 13+.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Prompta free?

Yes. The free version records in HD (720p) with no watermark and includes voice scrolling, voice commands, mirror mode and remote control. Scripts up to about 90 seconds scroll in full; a longer script can be unlocked for the day by watching a short ad, up to 3 times a day. Premium removes ads and the length limit and adds Full HD, 4K and word tracking.

Does Prompta add a watermark to my videos?

No. There is no watermark on any recording, in the free version or in Premium.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Prompta works without sign-up. Your scripts and recordings stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Does voice scrolling work offline?

Yes. Voice scrolling listens for your voice, not your words: the text moves while you speak and waits when you pause. It needs no internet and works in any language. Word tracking and voice commands use your device's speech recognition, which may need internet depending on your language.

What is the difference between voice scrolling and word tracking?

Voice scrolling (free) moves the text while you are speaking and stops when you pause. Word tracking (Premium) recognizes the words you read and keeps your current line at the reading line, word by word, even while recording.

Can I use Prompta with a teleprompter glass or a second camera?

Yes. Text mode turns Prompta into a prompter without the camera. Mirror or flip the text for beam-splitter glass, and control it with a Bluetooth clicker, keyboard or foot pedal.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later. Some features depend on the device: 4K recording, video stabilization, volume button control (iOS 17.2+) and, on Android, voice commands and word tracking (Android 13+).

What happens to my scripts if I cancel Premium?

Nothing is deleted. Your scripts and recordings stay on your device; only the Premium features stop. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play account.
