2What the app does

Scanelo turns paper into PDF files with your phone’s camera. It finds the edges of a page, straightens and cleans it, recognizes its text so you can search, copy, listen to or translate it, and names and sorts documents automatically. It makes real-size copies of ID cards, lets you add signatures, text and markup, and offers PDF tools such as merge, split, compress, image to PDF, PDF to images, page numbers, your own watermark and password protection, plus a QR code and barcode scanner. All of this happens on your phone; some features first need a model or language pack, which Google downloads once (see the Privacy Policy).

4Locked documents and your PIN

Locked documents are encrypted on your phone. We cannot open them or recover them for you. If you forget your security PIN, locked documents cannot be opened, unless fingerprint unlock is on, in which case you can unlock and set a new PIN. Backups keep locked documents encrypted with your PIN, so you need it to restore them. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes locked documents along with the rest of the app’s data.
