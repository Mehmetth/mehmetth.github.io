Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026 (2026-11-24) · Applies to: TrueScan for Android (com.durusoft.truescan), version 1.0 and later

The short version

  • Everything you scan stays on your device. Your documents, scans, PDFs and OCR text are processed and stored on your phone. TrueScan never uploads them to any server — ours or anyone else's.
  • No account, no login. We do not ask for your name or email to use the app. We cannot see what you scan.
  • Text recognition runs on your phone. ML Kit reads your pages on-device. The images are never sent to a recognition server.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob on the free plan, with consent managed by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where the law requires it. TrueScan Pro shows no ads.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1Who we are

TrueScan is an app for Android, published on Google Play by DuruSoft, based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For the limited personal data described here that we decide about, we are the data controller. Google acts as a separate controller or as our processors for the services they provide. You can reach us at duru.sofft@gmail.com.

TrueScan has no server of its own. It does not request your location, read your contacts or messages, or access files outside the ones you share with it.

Legal bases. Ads: your consent where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the free plan. Purchases: performing our contract with you. Analytics: your opt-in consent (you control this in Settings › Privacy). Messages you send us: answering your request. Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the same uses rely on Article 5(2)(c) and (f), and on your explicit consent under Article 5(1) where it is needed.

3What stays on your device

TrueScan stores and processes everything locally. We cannot access any of the following, and the app does not upload it:

  • Scans and PDFs you create are saved in the app's private storage and, when you export, to the location you choose.
  • OCR text recognised from your pages is stored locally so search and copy work offline.
  • Folder structure, tags, favourites and smart folder rules live in the app's local database.
  • Locked documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is held in Android Keystore and never leaves your device.
  • Signatures you draw are stored on your device only.
  • Settings and preferences are stored locally.

Uninstalling removes all local app data, including your documents, locked files and signatures. Export or back up anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

4Camera

TrueScan uses your camera only to scan documents. The camera preview and the photos taken are processed on your device to detect page edges, flatten the image, apply filters and produce the final scan. Raw camera frames are not stored to disk and are not sent anywhere. Access to the camera requires the CAMERA permission, which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time; the rest of the app continues to work for files you have already scanned.

5Text recognition and translation

TrueScan uses Google ML Kit for text recognition (OCR), barcode scanning and document translation. All recognition runs on your device — your document images are not sent to a recognition server. ML Kit may download language model files the first time you use a script or translation language; these downloads reveal your IP address to Google Play and information about the model requested. The model files are stored locally and reused for all subsequent recognitions.

See ML Kit Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy for details of what Google collects through the Play-services connection.

6Permissions and why

  • CAMERA: to scan documents.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: for ML Kit model downloads, purchases, ads and optional analytics.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional): trial reminders and backup completion alerts, created on your device.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC and USE_FINGERPRINT (optional): to unlock locked folders or documents. Android handles the biometric check; the app only receives a yes/no result.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: to process purchases through Google Play.
  • AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*: for advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox attribution, used by AdMob in the free plan.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: used by Play Asset Delivery when downloading large language packs in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK: used by WorkManager for background tasks such as automatic backup.

You can refuse or withdraw any optional permission in your device's Settings without losing core scanning features.

7Advertising and your consent

On the free plan, TrueScan shows ads from Google AdMob. Ad formats may include App Open, Interstitial, Rewarded, Native and Banner. Rewarded ads are always your choice. Ads never appear while you are actively scanning. Ads never use your documents, scans, OCR text or file names.

AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location it reveals, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. This can include personalized ads based on your interests.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and where required in US states, TrueScan asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before ads are personalized; "Do not consent" is a valid answer and the app works identically, with non-personalized ads where the law allows them. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy settings. You can also turn off ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads, and in your Google ad settings. TrueScan Pro users see no ads and the ad SDK does not start.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

8Purchases and subscriptions

TrueScan Pro (monthly, yearly and lifetime) is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes your payment; we never receive or store your card or payment information. The app reads your Pro status from Google Play on your device; no third party receives your purchase information and there is no TrueScan server. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

9Analytics

TrueScan may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (by Google) to collect anonymous usage events and crash reports. This feature is currently in a testing state; until real Firebase keys are configured, all events are written only to the device log. When enabled, the following may be collected: in-app interaction events (which features you use), setting names and values (not document content), error codes and performance timings, and crash reports with device and OS information.

No document content, file names, OCR text, folder names, or personal identifiers are included in any analytics event.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings › Privacy. When off, no events are sent and crash reports are suppressed. See Google's Privacy Policy.

10Device backup

If Android backup is turned on, your TrueScan user preferences (theme, language, default settings) may be included in device backups. Your documents, scans, PDFs, OCR text, locked files, folder structure and signatures are excluded from cloud backups and device transfers by design. You can back up your documents yourself using TrueScan's built-in ZIP backup feature, which saves to a location you choose (such as Google Drive).

11When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use this only to answer you and fix the issue you report, and we delete it when no longer needed, at the latest two years after our last exchange.

12Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data reaches only the services named in this policy, for the purposes stated. We work only with services that commit to protecting your data at least as well as this policy requires. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

13Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google and Firebase keep data under their own policies. There is no TrueScan account or server-side data to delete. For steps to delete your data, cancel a subscription or reset your advertising ID, see Delete your data.

14Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. If you are in Türkiye, you have the rights listed in KVKK Article 11. Because we hold very little about you, most of these actions are done on your device: delete documents or uninstall the app, change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy settings, or reset your advertising ID. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, write to us and we will help you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

15Children

TrueScan is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

16Security

Your documents and scans stay on your device. Locked documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and the encryption key is stored in the Android Keystore, which ties it to your device hardware. Your document lock PIN is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Connections to external services use HTTPS. No method is perfect, but we limit what leaves your device to the minimum described in this policy.

17International transfers

Google (AdMob, ML Kit, Firebase, Google Play) may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Your documents are never transferred internationally.

18Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we update the effective date above and notify you in the app.
