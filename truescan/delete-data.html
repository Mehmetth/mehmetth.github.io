Delete Your Data

Last updated: November 24, 2026

TrueScan has no account

TrueScan does not require a sign-in and does not hold any personal data on a server. All your documents, scans, PDFs and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting your data means clearing it from your device.

Delete all TrueScan data

Uninstalling the app removes everything TrueScan stored on your device: all documents, scans, PDFs, OCR text, locked files, signatures, folder structure, tags and preferences.

This cannot be undone. Export or back up any documents you want to keep before uninstalling. Use Profile › Backup in the app to save a ZIP to a location you choose.

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps (or Application manager, depending on your Android version).
  3. Find and tap TrueScan.
  4. Tap Uninstall and confirm.

Clear app data without uninstalling

To reset TrueScan to a fresh state while keeping the app installed:

This deletes all your documents and settings and cannot be undone. Back up first.

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps, then tap TrueScan.
  3. Tap Storage, then tap Clear data.

Cancel TrueScan Pro subscription

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

  1. Open Google Play on your device.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Tap Subscriptions, find TrueScan, and tap Cancel subscription.

Alternatively, in TrueScan tap Profile › Manage subscription.

Reset your advertising ID

Resetting your advertising ID disconnects past ad interactions from future ones. The path varies by device:

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Tap Google (or Privacy, or Ads — depends on your device).
  3. Tap Ads, then Reset advertising ID (or Delete advertising ID on Android 12+).

You can also manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.

Turn off analytics

To stop TrueScan from sending optional usage events:

  1. In TrueScan, tap Profile › Privacy.
  2. Turn off Share usage statistics.
