What it does

Scan, find, sort and sign

Everything happens on your phone, from the first photo to the finished PDF.

Scan to PDF in seconds

Live edge detection and auto capture take the shot when the page is steady. Batch scanning puts many pages into one PDF.

Find any word in any scan

Text recognition reads every page on your phone, so search finds the words inside your scans. Copy the text, listen to it or translate it.

Sorted on this phone

Each scan gets a clear name from its type and sender, such as an invoice or a contract. Smart folders gather receipts, IDs and notes by themselves.

Real-size ID card copies

Scan the front and back of an ID and get both sides on one page, ready to print or send. IDs the size of a bank card come out at real size.

Sign in a few taps

Draw your signature once and keep it for next time. Add initials, today's date, text, highlights and shapes.

Reads many scripts

Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, Bengali, Thai, Khmer, Chinese, Japanese and Korean text. Language models download once.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is TrueScan free?

Scanning, saving and sharing PDFs are free, with no watermark and no limit. Pro adds Word export, searchable and password-protected PDFs, redaction, unlimited signatures and translation, automatic backup and no ads.

Is there a watermark?

No, never, not even in the free version.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and scan.

Do my documents leave my phone?

No. Scans, text recognition and PDFs are made on your phone. Nothing is uploaded; a file leaves only when you share or save it somewhere yourself.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, editing, the PDF tools and search work without internet. Only the text recognition and QR scanner models, and optional language packs, download once.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Profile › Manage subscription opens your subscriptions in Google Play, where you can cancel in a few taps. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Use the same Google account and tap Restore purchases in Profile.

Is TrueScan available on iPhone?

TrueScan for iPhone and iPad is coming soon to the App Store. Today it is available for Android on Google Play.
