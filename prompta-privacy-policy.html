Prompta

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026 · Applies to the Prompta app for Android (com.durusoft.teleprompter.script)

In short
  • Prompta has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scripts, settings and recording list stay on your device.
  • The camera and microphone are used only while you record. Videos are saved to your device's gallery and are never uploaded by us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • If you buy Premium, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat verifies your subscription.

1. Who we are

Prompta ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by DuruSoft. For any privacy question or request, contact us at duru.sofft@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your device. We cannot see or access it.

DataWhy
Scripts, their titles and earlier versionsTo show, edit and restore your scripts.
Recording list (title, duration, date, link to the video file)To show your recordings in the app.
Preferences (text size, colors, speed, recording and remote-control settings)To remember how you like to use the app.

3. Camera and microphone

Prompta asks for camera and microphone access so that you can record video while reading your script. They are used only while the recording screen is open and a recording is being prepared or made. The resulting video is saved to your device's gallery (Movies/Prompta). We do not receive, upload or analyse your video or audio. While a recording is running, the app shows a notification so that you always know the camera and microphone are in use.

You can use the app without these permissions in the camera-free "text mode", and you can withdraw them at any time in your device settings.

4. Files and clipboard

When you choose to import a script, the app reads only the file you pick (TXT, Markdown or Word) or, if you tap "Paste from clipboard", the text currently in your clipboard. The text is saved on your device as a script. The clipboard is never read unless you ask for it.

5. Keyboards, remotes and pedals

If you connect a Bluetooth keyboard, presentation remote or foot pedal, or use your phone's volume buttons, the app reacts to the keys you press on the teleprompter screens. It does not record, store or send these key presses.

6. Purchases (Prompta Premium)

Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm that your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat processes:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (it is not linked to your name or email);
  • your purchase and subscription records from Google Play (product, dates, status, purchase token);
  • basic technical data sent with network requests, such as app version, operating system version, device language/country and IP address.

This data is used only to provide and restore your purchases and to prevent fraud. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. The "Support ID" shown in the app's settings is this anonymous app user ID; you can share it with us so that we can help with a purchase.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • Content leaves your device only when you choose to share it (for example with the system "Share" sheet).

8. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. You can delete all scripts, the recording list and your preferences at any time with Settings › Data & privacy › Delete my data in the app, or by uninstalling the app. Videos in your gallery are not deleted by the app; you can remove them with your gallery app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as described in its privacy policy; to request their deletion, contact us with your Support ID.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not keep your content on our servers, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app. For purchase-related data, contact us at duru.sofft@gmail.com.

10. Children

Prompta is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. Communication with Google Play and RevenueCat is encrypted (HTTPS). Please keep your device protected with a screen lock.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. We will change the "Effective date" above and, for significant changes, inform you in the app.
