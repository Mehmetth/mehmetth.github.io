Features

From the shelf to your Vault

Made for brick sets, die-cast cars and vinyl figures — at toy shows, garage sales, thrift stores or online.

  • Instant barcode & set-number scan

    CameraX and Google ML Kit read UPC/EAN barcodes and printed set or toy numbers on the device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. A photo from your gallery works too.

  • 47,505+ item offline catalog

    Search by code, name or series with no signal. Most box barcodes are not linked to a catalog entry yet: use the set number or the name, or jump straight to a sold-listings search.

  • Live sold comps, one tap

    Opens eBay Sold/Completed results for the condition you pick (MINT, CIB or LOOSE), or a Google Shopping search for retail prices, in your browser. No scraping and no paid price feed — you see the listings yourself.

  • Vault & gain/loss tracking

    Log condition, quantity and what you paid; enter the sold price you found to see gain or loss. Free up to 25 items, unlimited with Pro.

  • 1080×1920 value card

    Turn an item with a value into a story-sized card and share it with the apps on your phone. The card says whether the figure is a sample or one you entered.

  • Wanted list

    Save the pieces you are hunting and open their sold comps or a retail search from the Market tab.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Grailo has no server that stores your collection.

Where is my data stored?

On your device: your Vault, wanted list, entered prices and settings. If Android backup is on, Android may keep a copy in your own Google account. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Grailo tell me what my item is worth?

Not by itself. There is no price feed; only a handful of catalog items carry a clearly labelled sample value. Open the sold comps, then enter the price you found — Grailo uses it for gain and loss. Values are estimates, not an appraisal.

What if my barcode is not found?

That is common today: most barcodes are not linked to a catalog entry yet. Scan the set or toy number, search by name, or open a sold-listings search with the code. Vinyl figures are usually quickest to find by name.

Does it work offline?

Catalog search and your Vault work offline. The camera reader downloads once through Google Play services on first use. Sold comps, purchases and ads need a connection.

Are my camera pictures uploaded?

No. Camera frames and photos you pick are processed on the device to read codes and are not saved or uploaded.

How do I cancel Grailo Pro?

In Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Profile in the app. To avoid the yearly charge after a trial, cancel before the trial ends.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile → Delete all Vault data. Step-by-step instructions are on Delete Your Data.
